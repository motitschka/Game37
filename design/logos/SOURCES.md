# System logos: sources

The logos in this folder are trademarks of Nintendo and of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Game37 shows
them only to name the Systems whose Games it lists. They are not covered by Game37's GPL-3.0 licence,
and Game37 is not affiliated with or endorsed by either company.

Each `<system id>.svg` was downloaded unchanged from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-11-16, where it is marked
as public domain (too simple to be copyrighted). `design/make_logos.sh` renders them to the PNG files in
`assets/logos/`, turning black and near-black parts white so they read on the dark menus; coloured parts
keep their official colours.
